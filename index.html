HOW IT WORKS

Six stages. One traceable account.

Speak naturally. Preserve the original. Find the gaps. Face relevant perspectives. Leave with a clearer, evidence-linked preparation record.

01

Tell your account

Speak or type in your own language. Review and correct any transcript before it becomes part of the record.

02

Build the record

The original account, corrections, evidence, chronology, versions, sources and disputes stay distinct and traceable.

03

Find the gaps

Surface missing dates, unsupported assertions, contradictions, unclear attribution and unresolved questions — never a truth score.

04

Enter the room

Relevant perspectives are selected for the actual context rather than forcing every person through a fixed panel.

05

Face the questions

Questions respond to your account: revisit earlier answers, request evidence, expose missing context and test reasoning.

06

Leave with clarity

Receive a structured output covering support, uncertainty, alternative interpretations, missing evidence and practical preparation actions.

THE ROOM

A modern scrutiny environment. Not a theatrical courtroom.

You stay central. The room brings in only the perspectives that are relevant, shows why each is present, and makes disagreement visible rather than manufacturing consensus.

CANDOUR RULE

Witness Room does not simply validate you. It can say an answer has not addressed the question, that evidence does not establish the conclusion, or that another explanation exists.

THE ORVIA DISCIPLINE

Make certainty visible — and uncertainty visible too.

Across the case workspace and final report, Witness Room separates what is established from what is inferred, disputed, unknown, missing or still open.

What we know

Grounded in evidence or a verified source.

What we infer

A reasoned interpretation that is not the same as fact.

What is disputed

A material difference in account or interpretation.

What we do not know

An unanswered or unresolved matter.

What evidence is missing

Material that may resolve an important question.

What remains open

Questions or actions not yet closed.

SAFETY & BOUNDARIES

Preparation, not coaching. Assistance, not decision-making.

Witness Room can structure, question, compare and surface uncertainty. It does not determine guilt, credibility, safeguarding findings, diagnoses or legal liability.

Witness Room can

  • Structure an account
  • Identify missing information
  • Suggest questions for consideration
  • Compare accounts and evidence
  • Surface alternative hypotheses
  • Support simulated perspective review

Witness Room does not

  • Tell you what to say in court
  • Provide legal representation
  • Score truthfulness or credibility
  • Diagnose
  • Make final high-consequence decisions
  • Replace emergency or statutory services

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Know what is saved before you speak.

Choose a private practice session or intentionally save work to an ongoing case. The product must clearly explain retention, audio, transcription, access, export, correction, deletion, processors and human review.

Practice mode is designed for rehearsal without performative scoring or comparison against other users. Retention can be configured.
